## Clean Up Voice Recordings With Gleam Audio

[Gleam Audio](https://gleamaudio.com/) is an open-source desktop app that uses AI to clean voice recordings. It runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux, with all audio processing happening locally.

### Background Noise Removal

Gleam Audio uses an AI model trained on human speech to reduce background noise like hiss, hum, and fan noise. A simple slider lets you adjust the amount of noise removal, and batch processing makes it easy to clean multiple recordings at once, even offline.

### Audio Editing and Conversion

- Trim recordings using a visual waveform.
- Split long recordings at markers or into equal parts.
- Adjust loudness with presets for podcasts, audiobooks, streaming, and broadcast.
- Convert between formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, and Opus.

Gleam Audio is free for personal use up to 20 files per week. A pay-what-you-want license unlocks commercial use and unlimited processing.
